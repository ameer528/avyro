# Avyro

Avyro is an AI-powered client acquisition platform for freelancers and small agencies.

Stop spending hours looking for clients. Let AI find the opportunities while you focus on closing deals.

## What it does
- Analyzes your website and builds your business profile
- Researches competitors and your market
- Finds businesses that need your services, with evidence
- Identifies decision-makers and verified contact info
- Drafts personalized outreach for you to approve before sending
- Tracks prospects and follow-ups in a built-in CRM

## Tech stack
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, Gemini API

## Status
Phase 1 (in progress): authentication, onboarding, business profile.
